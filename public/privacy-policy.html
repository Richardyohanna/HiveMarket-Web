1. Information We Collect

HiveMarket collects information that is necessary to provide, maintain, secure, and improve our marketplace services. The information we collect depends on how you use HiveMarket.

1.1 Account Information

When you create or maintain a HiveMarket account, we may collect information such as:

  • Full name or display name.
  • Email address.
  • Phone number where provided.
  • Password or authentication credentials.
  • Account type and profile information.
  • Profile photo where provided.
  • Account preferences.
  • Verification information where required.

Authentication credentials are processed and stored using appropriate security measures and are not intended to be displayed publicly.

1.2 Location Information

HiveMarket may request access to your device's location to provide location-based marketplace features.

Location information may be used to:

  • Find shops near you.
  • Calculate approximate distances to shops.
  • Improve nearby shop discovery.
  • Provide location-relevant marketplace recommendations.

HiveMarket does not use continuous background location tracking for ordinary marketplace use unless such functionality is specifically introduced and disclosed to users.

1.3 Shop and Seller Information

If you register or manage a shop on HiveMarket, we may collect information such as:

  • Shop name.
  • Shop description.
  • Shop category.
  • Shop address or general location.
  • Operating hours.
  • Contact information.
  • Shop images and logos.
  • Products and services.
  • Prices and discounts.
  • Seller profile information.

Some shop and listing information may be publicly visible to other HiveMarket users because marketplace discovery is a core function of the service.

1.4 Product and Listing Information

When users or sellers create marketplace listings, HiveMarket may collect:

  • Product or service name.
  • Product or service description.
  • Product images.
  • Price information.
  • Discount information.
  • Category information.
  • Availability information.
  • Associated shop information.

1.5 Messages and Communications

HiveMarket may process information associated with communications between users and sellers through the platform.

This may include:

  • Message content.
  • Message timestamps.
  • Conversation participants.
  • Attachments or media where supported.
  • Information required to deliver notifications.

Messages may be processed to provide the messaging functionality, deliver notifications, maintain security, prevent abuse, and troubleshoot technical problems.

1.6 Search, Views, Saves, and Marketplace Activity

HiveMarket may collect information about how users interact with marketplace content.

This may include:

  • Search queries.
  • Products viewed.
  • Shops viewed.
  • Saved shops.
  • Saved products where supported.
  • Cart or shopping-list activity.
  • Categories viewed.
  • Marketplace interactions.

This information may be used to improve search results, recommendations, trending content, and the overall HiveMarket experience.

1.7 Device and Technical Information

When you use HiveMarket, we may automatically receive certain technical information, including:

  • Device type.
  • Operating system.
  • Application version.
  • Device or installation identifiers where applicable.
  • IP address.
  • Network information.
  • Error and diagnostic information.
  • Push notification tokens.

Technical information helps us operate, secure, diagnose, and improve the application.

1.8 Push Notifications

If you enable notifications, HiveMarket may process a push notification token associated with your device.

Push notifications may be used for messages, account activity, shop activity, listing updates, marketplace activity, and other relevant service notifications.

You can control notification permissions through your device settings and, where available, through HiveMarket settings.

2. How We Use Information

HiveMarket may use collected information for the following purposes:

  • Creating and managing user accounts.
  • Authenticating users.
  • Providing marketplace functionality.
  • Displaying shops, products, and services.
  • Providing nearby shop discovery.
  • Calculating approximate distances.
  • Processing marketplace searches.
  • Providing personalized recommendations.
  • Supporting user-to-seller communications.
  • Delivering push notifications.
  • Allowing sellers to manage shops and listings.
  • Processing reports and abuse complaints.
  • Preventing fraud, abuse, and unauthorized activity.
  • Protecting the security of HiveMarket.
  • Monitoring and diagnosing technical problems.
  • Improving application performance and usability.
  • Understanding marketplace trends.
  • Complying with applicable legal obligations.
  • Providing customer and technical support.

We only use information for purposes that are reasonably related to operating, improving, securing, and providing HiveMarket services, or as otherwise permitted or required by applicable law.

3. Personalized Recommendations

HiveMarket may use information about your interactions with the marketplace to provide more relevant recommendations.

Depending on the features available to you, recommendation systems may consider information such as:

  • Products you view.
  • Shops you view.
  • Searches you perform.
  • Categories you interact with.
  • Saved products or shops.
  • Cart or shopping-list activity.
  • General marketplace activity.
  • Approximate location and distance.
  • Popular or trending marketplace content.

These signals may be used to rank and recommend shops, products, categories, or other marketplace content that may be relevant to you.

Recommendation systems may change as HiveMarket develops new features and improves its marketplace algorithms.

4. How We Share Information

HiveMarket does not sell users' personal information as a business practice.

We may share or make information available in the following circumstances:

4.1 Public Marketplace Information

Information that you intentionally publish as part of a public marketplace listing may be visible to other users and visitors.

This may include shop names, product names, listing descriptions, prices, images, general shop location, categories, seller information, and other information that you choose to publish.

4.2 Service Providers

We may use third-party companies and service providers to help operate HiveMarket. These providers may process information on our behalf for services such as hosting, databases, authentication, media storage, notifications, analytics, email delivery, infrastructure, security, and other technical services.

4.3 Legal Requirements

We may disclose information when we reasonably believe disclosure is necessary to comply with applicable laws, regulations, legal processes, court orders, government requests, or to protect the rights, safety, and security of HiveMarket, our users, or others.

4.4 Business Transfers

If HiveMarket is involved in a merger, acquisition, restructuring, financing, sale of assets, or similar business transaction, information may be transferred as part of that transaction, subject to applicable law and appropriate protections.

5. Third-Party Services

HiveMarket may rely on third-party services and infrastructure to operate certain parts of the application.

Depending on the features and configuration of HiveMarket, these services may include:

  • Firebase services.
  • Supabase services.
  • Cloudinary or other media storage services.
  • Cloud and application hosting providers.
  • Email delivery providers.
  • Push notification infrastructure.
  • Database and infrastructure providers.

Third-party providers may process information according to their own privacy policies and contractual obligations.

HiveMarket takes reasonable steps to use reputable service providers and configure services appropriately for the protection of user information.

6. Data Security

HiveMarket takes reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, misuse, or destruction.

Security measures may include authentication controls, access restrictions, encrypted connections, secure infrastructure, controlled database access, and other appropriate safeguards.

However, no internet transmission, electronic storage system, or software application can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Therefore, we cannot guarantee absolute security of your information.

You are responsible for protecting your account credentials and should notify HiveMarket if you believe your account has been accessed without authorization.

7. Data Retention

HiveMarket retains information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the services, maintain accounts, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, enforce agreements, maintain security, prevent fraud and abuse, and fulfill legitimate operational purposes.

Different types of information may be retained for different periods depending on the purpose for which the information was collected and applicable legal requirements.

When information is no longer required for legitimate purposes, HiveMarket may delete, anonymize, or otherwise dispose of the information in accordance with applicable requirements and our operational processes.

8. Account Deletion

HiveMarket provides users with a method to request deletion of their account and associated personal information.

Users may initiate an account deletion request through the account-management functionality provided by HiveMarket or by contacting HiveMarket using the contact information provided in this Privacy Policy.

When an account deletion request is received, HiveMarket will process the request in accordance with applicable law and its legitimate operational requirements.

Account deletion may result in the removal of account information, profile information, marketplace activity associated with the account, and other personal information where applicable.

Certain information may be retained where necessary to comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, prevent fraud or abuse, maintain security, enforce agreements, or satisfy other legitimate legal requirements.

Information that has been anonymized or aggregated so that it can no longer reasonably be associated with an individual may also be retained.
