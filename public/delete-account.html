Delete Your HiveMarket Account

HiveMarket provides users with a way to permanently delete their account and associated personal data.

Account deletion can be performed directly from the HiveMarket mobile application by an authenticated account owner.

For users who can access their account: You can delete your account directly from the HiveMarket app without contacting support.

How to delete your HiveMarket account

  1. Open the HiveMarket mobile application.
  2. Sign in to your HiveMarket account.
  3. Open your Profile or account settings.
  4. Select Delete Account.
  5. Review the deletion information and confirm that you want to permanently delete your account.

Why do I need to sign in?

Account deletion requires authentication to protect users from unauthorized deletion of their accounts.

Only the authenticated account owner can initiate the account deletion action through the HiveMarket application.

What data is deleted?

When an account deletion is successfully completed, HiveMarket deletes or removes personal information associated with the account where applicable.

Data Deletion
Account information Deleted or removed
Profile information Deleted or removed
Saved items and personal preferences Deleted or removed where applicable
Personal information associated with the account Deleted or removed where applicable
User-generated listings or content Removed or anonymized where applicable

Data that may be retained

Certain information may be retained when necessary to comply with applicable legal requirements, resolve disputes, prevent fraud or abuse, maintain security, or satisfy legitimate record-keeping obligations.

Where information is required to be retained, HiveMarket will retain only the information necessary for the applicable purpose and for the period required by that purpose.

Account deletion is permanent.

Once your account deletion has been completed, your account may not be recoverable. Make sure you have saved any information you may need before confirming deletion.

Can't access your account?

If you cannot sign in to your HiveMarket account and therefore cannot use the in-app account deletion feature, contact HiveMarket support.

Request assistance with account deletion

Contact HiveMarket support using the email address associated with your account. You may be asked to provide additional information to verify that you are the account owner before the deletion request can be processed.

support@hivemarket.ng
